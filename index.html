✦ About the Ministry

Abigael Glory Ministries was founded by Besong Aseck Lydia — a Spirit-filled Digital Christian Evangelist with an unquenchable burden for souls. Born out of personal hardship, she launched her Facebook ministry in 2025, quickly reaching over 5,000 followers across Cameroon and beyond.

Through Gospel Blast, teaching series on the Life of Jesus, Journey to Easter, and upcoming books Abigael and Sophia, the ministry reaches the world — offering free books, premium resources, humanitarian grants, AI-powered prayer support, and direct evangelism across prisons, orphanages and communities in need.
